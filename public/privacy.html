Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

PSE Stocks Watchlist by Nialo is a free website for following Philippine Stock Exchange prices. It has no accounts, no sign-up, no ads and no cookies, and we don't sell or share your information. This page explains exactly what the site handles.

Stored only on your device

The site saves these in your browser's local storage so they're there when you come back:

  • your watchlist, its sections and their order;
  • portfolio positions you enter (number of shares and average cost) and your fee settings;
  • display settings: refresh interval, sorting, Prices/Portfolio view, light/dark mode and whether the Index Summary is expanded.

Your portfolio positions never leave your device. To delete this data, remove the items in the app, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Sent to our server

To show prices, your browser asks our server for data, and our server fetches it from PSE Edge. These requests include the stock symbols on your watchlist and anything you type in the search box. We don't store them; the server only keeps market data briefly in memory, and error logs may note which stock symbol failed to load.

Like any website, our hosting provider (Vercel) receives standard request information such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request, which it handles under its own privacy policy. Your browser does not contact PSE Edge directly unless you click a link to it.

Visitor statistics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It records the page visited, the referring site, approximate location (such as country), and device, browser and operating system type. It uses no cookies: visitors are recognised only by a hash of the request that is discarded after 24 hours, and we see only totals, never individual visitors.

Your rights

We don't collect your name, email or other personal details. If you have a question or request about your data, including under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, contact the site owner, Nialo.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
